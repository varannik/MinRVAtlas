## 1. What are Isometric's stages from first contact to first credit issuance, and what is the entry/exit gate for each?

### Takeaway
Isometric publishes a nine-step path: (1) optional eligibility assessment → (2) sign Supplier Agreement + project appendix (unlocks full Certify and a Registry Operations Manager) → (3) create project in Certify + issuance forecast → (4) PDD + LCA drafted in Certify (Remi AI checks) → (5) Isometric pre-screen → (6) 10-day public comment on the redacted PDD → (7) first GHG statement submitted → (8) VVB validation incl. site visit → (9) Isometric reviews the VVB's final report, project verified, certificates issued and listed. Validation and the first verification are done as one combined VVB engagement; the site visit normally happens *after* the first GHG statement is submitted. Isometric's planning figure is ~3 months from first PDD draft to first issuance for an already-operating project, with the final V&V report typically 6–8 weeks after the site visit.

### Cited Findings
- Official step list (headings verbatim): "1. Eligibility assessment" — optional: "Isometric reviews your existing project design documents and issues an eligibility report, to establish protocol alignment before signing the project"; "2. Sign the Supplier Agreement and project appendix" — "After signing, your project is committed to issuing with Isometric. You will be assigned a Registry Operations Manager to support with Validation and Verification"; "3. Create the project in Certify" and "add an issuance forecast"; "4. Prepare and submit Project Design and LCA" (requirements checklist + LCA from protocol template, both screenable by Remi); "5. Isometric pre-screen"; "6. Public comment period (10 days)" — PDD "published, with agreed redactions, on the Registry for 10 days. Isometric reviews public feedback before sharing it with you"; "7. Prepare and submit GHG statement" for the first reporting period; "8. VVB Validation and site visit"; "9. Verification report, and certificate issuance" — "Isometric reviews the VVB's final report. When no amendments remain, the project is verified. Isometric issues certificates and lists them on the Registry." — [Onboarding, process and timelines FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/working-with-isometric/onboarding-and-timelines.md)
- Timelines: "For an operational project that has already completed creditable activities, we advise planning around a three month timeline, from submission of a first Project Design draft." "After the site visit has been completed, the final Validation and Verification report is typically issued within 6-8 weeks, though this can depend on the project pathway." After signing, the Registry Operations Team "will share a proposed Validation and Verification schedule with interim milestones." — [Onboarding FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/working-with-isometric/onboarding-and-timelines.md)
- Certify's V&V stages: (1) V&V kick-off with the Registry Operations Manager (ROM), "your main point of contact throughout the Validation & Verification (V&V) process — all the way through to credit issuance" (walks process, introduces Certify, aligns timelines/milestones); (2) pre-screen: supplier submits PDD and LCA through Certify; Isometric scientists review, comment and "Iterate together until everything meets the Isometric Standard and relevant protocol"; (3) PDD 10-day public comment period on the Registry; feedback reviewed by Isometric "before sharing with you and the VVB, after which amendments are recommended"; (4) VVB selection: "Once the pre-screen process has progressed, Isometric sends out a request for proposals (RFP)"; (5) validation: VVB validation report, "which Isometric reviews and publishes onto the registry page"; (6) verification of the GHG Statement submitted in Certify ("assesses historical data"); (7) credit issuance "After the Validation & Verification reports have been reviewed by Isometric and the project proponent and no outstanding amendments remain". — [Validation and verification](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/validation.md)
- PDD gate mechanics in Certify: progress bar lists tasks blocking the next stage (e.g. "Complete all requirements"); "For requirements where Remi is available, you must run it and address issues during drafting"; Remi "Issues must be resolved or disputed in order to submit the Project Design for pre-screen review"; "When all remaining tasks are completed, the **Request validation** button ... will be enabled. You can then submit your PDD draft for pre-screen review. Isometric will review your submission and, once all issues are addressed, send it to the verifier for validation." Supplier marks each issue addressed or disputed and re-submits. — [Project design](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/project-design.md)
- Pre-screen only starts on a complete PDD: "Isometric conducts the pre-screen review once the PDD is finalized and submitted." Submissions are then "approved iteratively as issues are addressed." — [Onboarding FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/working-with-isometric/onboarding-and-timelines.md)
- LCA gate: "Once an LCA draft is finalized it is submitted to Isometric for pre-screen. After any noted issues are resolved during pre-screen, it will be passed on to the verifier assigned to your project for validation. Ex-ante estimates can be provided for LCA validation if your project is not yet fully operational." Data-check issues "are not gating to submission" but "must be resolved before your LCA is validated." — [Build an LCA](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/lca.md)
- Issue gate: "All issues on a particular submission must be resolved before it can be validated or verified, unless the issue is a Forward Action Request." Issue types follow ISO 14064-3: Clarification Request, Error, Misreport, Misrepresentation, Nonconformity, Omission. Issues are raised by Isometric at pre-screen and by the verifier at validation/verification; email digests every 24 h to Admin/Editor users. — [Log of Issues](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/log-of-issues.md)
- Site visit: mandatory for validation, "must happen before a project is Validated, and therefore before any certificates are issued"; VVB must see business-as-usual operations; "The visit usually takes place after you submit your GHG statement"; "one to two days per key location"; valid "for the duration of your crediting period, unless your project changes materially". VVB engagement sequence: kick-off and planning (VVB shares V&V plan) → audit and desk review (PDD, MRV plan, LCA, evidence) → site visit → follow-ups (findings) → closing ("the VVB submits a final report for Isometric to review. Isometric then validates the project and verifies the certificates"). — [VVB site visit FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/validation-and-verification/site-visits.md)
- BioCCS protocol: site visits to capture and, where applicable, storage facilities are required at least during the first Validation or Verification and at least once in each Validation; more visits after substantial operational changes or at Isometric's/VVB's discretion. The In-situ Mineralization module states the same. — [BioCCS Protocol v1.5](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/biogenic-capture-and-storage/1.5); [In-situ Mineralization Module v1.2](https://registry.isometric.com/module/in-situ-mineralization)
- Standard: 10-day public comment before registration, "extends to 30 days if material comments arrive or a stakeholder asks for more time" (§3.3; shortened to 10 days in v2.1); after Isometric approves the validation report "the Project is listed as Validated with an Approval Date"; once a verification report is accepted the units are Verified and eligible for issuance; certificates are issued to the Project Proponent. VVB verifies each GHG Statement under ISO 14064-3 at reasonable assurance. — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard); [Standard changelog](https://registry.isometric.com/standard/2.2/changelog)
- VVB report quality gate: "Verification and validation audit reports will receive a quality review by Isometric prior to the issuance of credits... Credits will only be issued once the report meets a satisfactory level of quality." — [Isometric VVB Policy v1.2, 25.07.2025](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KOmLuhU6yzCYYtUWPyQCw4aByEEJ7Q1-/view)
- Late entry: registering after production has started is possible "provided that the project meets the relevant Protocol requirements"; Isometric "typically only does this for projects with offtakes in place, or that meet minimum tonnage thresholds. For a pilot-scale project without those, you would need to agree commitments before Isometric takes on certification without reserved time in advance." — [Onboarding FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/working-with-isometric/onboarding-and-timelines.md)
- Explanatory Notes (where a requirement does not fit): three uses — planned protocol update, later compliance recorded as a Forward Action Request with a deadline, or an agreed alternative approach ("last resort"); approved and published by Isometric; "only issued as exceptions and at Isometric's discretion". — [Audit exemptions and Explanatory Notes FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/validation-and-verification/audit-exemptions-and-explanatory-notes.md)
- Marketing claim (not a process guarantee): "Most suppliers can issue certificates within a month of getting started"; "Certificates will be issued each month after certification". — [isometric.com/suppliers](https://isometric.com/suppliers)

### Inferences
- Practical gate map: Supplier Agreement signed → Preview restrictions lifted, ROM assigned, V&V schedule issued. Entry to pre-screen = all PDD rows complete + Remi issues resolved/disputed + "Request validation". Exit from pre-screen = all Isometric issues closed (PDD and LCA) → redacted PDD published (public comment) and VVB RFP launched. VVB starts only after Isometric contracts it. Site visit generally after the first GHG statement is in Certify. Exit = Isometric accepts the VVB's combined validation + verification report → project "Validated" + first issuance.
- The "~1 month" marketing claim conflicts with the FAQ's ~3-month planning figure; the FAQ figure is the one to plan around, and a new storage-site design (in-situ mineralisation) will likely take longer than an operating biochar-type project.

### Gaps
- No public checklist for the optional eligibility assessment (scope, cost, duration).
- No published service levels for pre-screen turnaround or RFP duration.

## 2. What agreements are signed, and what KYC / due diligence does Isometric run on suppliers?

### Takeaway
The supplier signs one commercial contract: a **Supplier Agreement** plus a **project appendix** per project; signing commits the project to issue with Isometric. Registry access comes with the Certify account (no separate registry contract for suppliers is described). Isometric's published KYC Policy (v1.1, July 2024) applies to suppliers and buyers alike: legal name, registered address, company number and sector, screened against FATF high-risk jurisdictions, government watchlists and UN sanctions. The Standard also requires legal ownership evidence and contracts with other project participants.

### Cited Findings
- "Sign the Supplier Agreement and project appendix. After signing, your project is committed to issuing with Isometric." A partner company "can be named in the PDD even where only one entity signs the appendix", and can be given Certify access. — [Onboarding FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/working-with-isometric/onboarding-and-timelines.md)
- Preview accounts: "Speak to our Partnerships team to discuss your Supplier Agreement and unlock all features." — [Certify introduction](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/introduction.md)
- Supplier listing "published to the Registry during onboarding of a Supplier after receipt of a Supplier Agreement. On request, publication of the listing may be postponed until the start of the public comment period for a Supplier's first project." — [Data visibility](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/data-visibility.md)
- KYC Policy (Version 1.1: 08.07.2024; v1.0: 14.12.2023): applies to "any Account Holders on the Isometric Registry... both Buyers of credits, as well as the originating Project Proponents ('Suppliers')". Data collected: "Company name (full legal name); Company registered address; Company number; Industry sector". Isometric will "Check that Account Holders are not based in FATF high-risk jurisdictions, named on government sponsored watchlists or international (UN) sanctions lists. If any of these triggers are met, an account cannot be opened"; "Build an understanding of the nature and legitimacy of Account Holders' businesses"; keep KYC records ≥5 years from the most recent transaction; monitor transactions. On suspicious activity Isometric may cease trading, identify and screen beneficial owners, or make an on-site visit. Isometric states it is not itself in scope of AML regulation but follows UK Money Laundering Regulations / JMLSG as best practice. — [Isometric KYC Policy v1.1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YLopoLcxZojb1-6rO8d6bzLwW05cIkdX/view) (linked from [Governance & policies](https://isometric.com/governance-policies))
- Standard §3.1: contracts with supply-chain and logistics parties must transfer ownership of Removals to the Project Proponent and give data access; evidence of legal ownership includes a representation to Isometric and contracts with other project participants. — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- BioCCS: "a single Project Proponent must be specified contractually as the sole owner to avoid double counting." — [BioCCS protocol (registry)](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/biogenic-capture-and-storage)
- Certificates "may only be delivered to organizations with an active Isometric account". — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- Other governance documents on the same page: Anti-Bribery and Corruption, Conflicts of Interest, Grievance, Environmental and Social, VVB Policy, CRCF Policy, CORSIA Eligibility Policy, CORSIA Supplier Insurance Policy, Letter of Authorization template (CORSIA). — [Governance & policies](https://isometric.com/governance-policies)

### Inferences
- For a capture-at-cement-site, truck, third-party-storage chain, the ownership/representation rules mean the PDD must carry contracts from the cement host, haulier and storage operator assigning the removal claim and data access to the single Project Proponent; a partner can be named without signing the appendix.
- If credits are wanted as CORSIA-eligible, a Letter of Authorization from the host country and the CORSIA policies would add steps; not required for voluntary issuance.

### Gaps
- The Supplier Agreement and project appendix texts are not public (term, exclusivity, termination, liability for reversals, data licence). Only the KYC policy and Standard obligations are public.
- The KYC policy on the site is dated 2024 (v1.1); no newer version was found.

## 3. How is the VVB chosen and contracted, who pays, and what are Isometric's fees?

### Takeaway
Isometric — not the supplier — runs a public RFP among its approved VVBs, selects, contracts and pays the VVB; the supplier can state a preference but cannot contract directly. The **buyer** pays Isometric a per-tonne certification fee on delivery that covers VVB costs; there are no registration, issuance, account, transfer or retirement fees for suppliers. The only supplier-side charge is an unpublished flat fee for issuances below 1,000 certificates. BioCCS sits in the "Biomass Carbon Removal and Storage" pathway, which uses the "Closed system" fee schedule: $10/t for a supplier issued <25,000 t under that protocol in the prior year, falling to $3/t above 500,000 t.

### Cited Findings
- "Isometric contracts and pays the VVB on your behalf. We will run a public request for proposals (RFP) that any onboarded Validation and Verification Body (VVB) can bid on." Selection criteria: knowledge of the pathway and processes; familiarity with the region; availability, speed and quality; pricing proposal. "Once contracting is finalized, Isometric will introduce a Supplier to the appointed VVB for a Validation kickoff." — [VVB selection FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/validation-and-verification/vvb-selection.md)
- "Can I contract the VVB directly myself? No: Isometric contracts and pays the VVB, so the verifier has no financial relationship with the project developer." Supplier preference "will be considered among the selection criteria in the competitive RFP, as long as the organization satisfies other criteria with their bid." Approved VVB list is on the governance page. — [VVB selection FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/validation-and-verification/vvb-selection.md)
- Standard §1.2.2: Isometric "independently and impartially selects third-party VVBs" and pays for verification; it charges Buyers "a single flat fee per Offtake or purchase", based on pathway MRV effort, "not on the number of Certificates issued"; it "does not receive payments from Project Proponents to verify deliveries" and "does not sell or broker Certificates". — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- VVB eligibility: accreditation by an IAF member against ISO 14065 (or ISO 14034/17020/17029), or a governmental/intergovernmental body, or case-by-case compliance with ISO 19011; audit team must include a team leader plus a separate validator/verifier (no sole proprietors); VVBs complete Isometric onboarding; a VVB that helped develop the project cannot audit it; VVB signs a conflict-of-interest declaration per statement of work. Rotation: a project may use one VVB for no more than five consecutive years, and a VVB may verify a project in no more than five of any seven consecutive years. — [VVB Policy v1.2](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KOmLuhU6yzCYYtUWPyQCw4aByEEJ7Q1-/view); [Isometric Standard v2.2 §4.1–4.5](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- Fees: "Isometric charges buyers directly, rather than suppliers." "Buyers pay a per-tonne certification fee, which is paid on delivery. This fee varies by pathway." "A flat fee is chargeable to suppliers for issuances below 1,000 certificates. This fee offsets verification costs that smaller issuances would not otherwise cover." "Isometric's single certification fee covers everything—including VVB costs. No annual account fees, secondary transaction fees, or retirement fees." Tiers set annually from 1 January on prior-calendar-year issuance; from 1 Jan 2030 fees index to US CPI. — [Isometric pricing](https://isometric.com/pricing)
- Fee schedule (Isometric's published calculator spreadsheet): "Pricing is set per Supplier, per protocol"; "Prices apply to every tonne procured in the year. Bands are not blended"; "Verification fee = verification price x tonnes procured". Pathway "Biomass Carbon Removal and Storage" (sub-header "Biochar, BioCCS and Biomass Storage") → "Closed system" schedule: $10 (0–<25k t), $9 (25k–50k), $8 (50k–75k), $7 (75k–100k), $6 (100k–150k), $5 (150k–200k), $4 (200k–250k), $3.5 (250k–300k), $3.4, $3.3, $3.2, $3.1, $3 (≥500k). "Carbon Mineralization" pathway → "Open system" schedule starting at $30/t (<5k t). Direct Air Capture → Closed system. — [Isometric verification fee calculator (xlsx)](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65ef0b18cfe3fb84ec002a81/6af2132e3d8a4d27b0d81555_Isometric%20verification%20fee%20calculator.xlsx), linked from [pricing](https://isometric.com/pricing)
- Orders: "An order is logged in the Isometric registry once a buyer has also agreed a verification fee with Isometric." — [Key Registry Concepts](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/registry/key-registry-concepts.md)
- No commission: Isometric "does not charge any kind of commission or brokerage fee" when connecting suppliers with buyers. — [Buyers, sales and market FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/working-with-isometric/buyers-and-market.md)

### Inferences
- A BioCCS + in-situ mineralisation project should be priced on the Closed-system ($10/t first-tier) schedule because the fee follows the protocol (BioCCS), not the storage module; confirm with Isometric because "Carbon Mineralization" is a separate, more expensive open-system schedule.
- Supplier out-of-pocket costs are therefore its own: PDD/LCA preparation, consultants, labs, permits, monitoring, site-visit hosting, plus the small-issuance flat fee if an issuance is <1,000 t.

### Gaps
- The amount of the supplier flat fee for issuances <1,000 certificates is not published.
- No public figure for VVB cost per engagement (Isometric absorbs it).
- Whether the eligibility assessment is charged is not stated.

## 4. How does Certify structure a project, and what must be set up before the first GHG statement?

### Takeaway
Certify hierarchy: Organization (roles) → Project (pathway, protocol, modules, settings) → Project Design (PDD requirement checklist) → [Feedstocks] → Storage sites (validation requirements + reversal-monitoring plan + monitoring submissions) → LCA (project operations components from blueprints + project emissions with amortisation) converted into a GHG entry template → GHG entries (each net-negative; one credit batch each; vintage = end-date year) → GHG statements (contiguous reporting periods, sensitivity analysis on the first, narrative report link) → submit for verification. Before the first statement, the project needs a submitted PDD and LCA (pre-screened), a frozen template, project emissions, storage site(s) with a monitoring plan and submissions covering the period, and GHG entries whose end dates fall in the period.

### Cited Findings
- Create project: title, short description (≤400 characters), country; select pathway and protocol ("Contact your Partnership Manager if you need to set up a project for a protocol that is not yet certified"); select modules ("Any required modules will be selected automatically"); Overview page shows "outstanding tasks required ... for your project validation and first credit issuance". Extra settings exist only for some protocols (Biochar, Enhanced Weathering); "Settings are locked once your project has been validated or relevant data has been uploaded." — [Create a project](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/create-project.md)
- PDD requirements derive from the Standard, the protocol and the modules; each carries a shortcode (e.g. `R-NK7R-0`); sections: Project setup; Protocol & monitoring; Environmental & social; Stakeholder input; Pathway specific. Source uploads need a category (Plan/Assessment, Technical Drawing, Specification, Lab Result, Operational Record, Model/Calculation, Permit/Certificate, Contract/Affidavit, Stakeholder Record, Reference/Evidence, Other), reason and pages. — [Project design](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/project-design.md)
- Every sub-requirement must be explicitly answered; attachments should not replace written explanations. — [PDD quality best practice FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/validation-and-verification/pdd-quality-best-practice.md)
- "There is no single standalone template" for PDD/LCA; Isometric recommends "a free Certify trial account and creating a draft project to see all the project design requirements", which can be exported for offline review. Three data routes: Certify UI forms/CSV bulk upload; API integration; third-party dMRV partner. Third-party dMRV is not required; in-house dMRV is reviewed at pre-screen and validation. — [Certify and data FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/platform/certify-and-data.md)
- Storage sites: for **BioCCS** a storage site is "A geological formation into which captured CO₂ is injected". Each site needs name, unique reference ID, storage method, geometry. Site "Requirements" tab = validation requirements; "Reversal monitoring" tab = monitoring plan (phase: Operation/Pre-operation/Post-operation; parameter; frequency; method). "Monitoring requirements are generated automatically for each storage site when it is created" — but submissions "are currently configured" only for salt cavern, subsurface biomass, permeable reservoirs and depleted hydrocarbon reservoir modules; saline aquifer "will be supported shortly". Submissions carry a source file, a validity period and notes; they are pulled into any GHG statement whose period they cover; statement Monitoring tab shows Valid / Partial / Missing per site. — [Storage sites](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/storage-sites.md)
- Project assets (sites/activities/materials) framework is "[beta]" and currently supported only for Reforestation, IFM, Bio-oil Geological Storage, Improved Soil Management, Enhanced Weathering in Agriculture and Agroforestry; "Other protocols will be migrated." Validatable sites allow later project expansion, validated by the verifier at the first verification of credits from that site. — [Project assets [beta]](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/project-assets/introduction.md)
- Feedstock tab (draft feedstock, eligibility criteria, evidence, a "feedstock appendix that will be added to your PDD") is supported for "BiCRS projects using the Biomass Feedstock Accounting module" and EW. — [Add feedstocks](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/add-feedstock.md)
- LCA: "an ex-ante carbon accounting projection"; start from the protocol default template; normalise by functional unit or sample time period ("better suited to ... engineered removals"); components built from blueprints (e.g. truck journey = Transport emissions blueprint `mass * distance * emission_factor`, or Fuel usage by volume if fuel is metered); project emissions (establishment/end-of-life) allocated to the first statement or amortised; run data checks; then "Convert into a new template" for GHG entries. For credit verifications "monitored inputs require ex-post measured values for the specific reporting period as well as supporting evidence". API: prefer `ghg_entry*` / `ghg_entry_template*`; `removal*` endpoints are deprecated aliases. — [Build an LCA](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/lca.md); [Certify API introduction](https://docs.isometric.com/api-reference/certify/certify-introduction.md)
- GHG entries "must be individually creditable: that is, each must have a net negative total"; examples: "A CDR process with a product injection stage might report data per injection"; "After issuance, each GHG entry will correspond to a distinct batch of credits"; vintage = year of the entry's end date; an entry joins the statement whose period contains its end date. "GHG statement reporting periods form a single unbroken timeline"; no overlap, no gaps; new entries cannot be submitted into an already-verified period. — [GHG Entries](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/ghg-entry.md)
- GHG statement steps: create draft → set period → add GHG entries → optional reporting-period emissions → sensitivity analysis on the project's first statement → written GHG statement report on a shared drive visible to verifier and Isometric → data checks (not gating but must be resolved before verification) → "Submit GHG statement" with report link. Amendments create a new statement version for resubmission. — [GHG statements](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/ghg-statement.md)
- Sources are private by default, shared with the verifier of the related GHG entry; "Verifiers are not notified when new sources are added ... Please notify the Isometric Registry Operations team". — [Key Certify Concepts](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/key-certify-concepts.md)
- Roles: Admin (users, Registry deliveries/retirements/transfers, forecasts, API tokens), Editor (PDD, LCA, GHG statement, datapoints), Viewer, Custom access (named projects only, e.g. consultants). — [Account roles](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/account-roles.md)
- Issuance forecast: required coverage of the next 24 months per project (Month/Quarter/Year cadence; "First certificate target"); placeholder projects allowed before Certify setup; Admin role needed; rollout may need enabling via support. — [Create issuance forecast](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/registry/create-issuance-forecast.md)
- APIs: Certify (MRV) API and Registry API, OpenAPI specs at `https://api.isometric.com/mrv/v0/mrv.openapi.json` and `https://api.isometric.com/registry/v0/openapi.json`; no SDKs; production `https://api.isometric.com/`, sandbox `https://api.sandbox.isometric.com/`; "Accounts and API credentials are independent across the two environments." — [API introduction](https://docs.isometric.com/api-reference/introduction.md). Auth = `X-Client-Secret` (per environment, no expiry) + org `Authorization: Bearer` JWT (expires 1 year after generation), both managed in Certify Settings. — [Authentication](https://docs.isometric.com/api-reference/authentication.md)
- An Isometric MCP server lets AI assistants query protocols and the organisation's data and act in Certify/Registry. — [docs index](https://docs.isometric.com/llms.txt) (entry "MCP server")

### Inferences
- For this project: one Certify project under BioCCS v1.5 with the In-situ Mineralization module; the injection well/formation is the storage site; the cement capture unit, liquefaction and trucking live in the LCA (energy, fuel/transport, embodied emissions components) rather than as Certify "sites" (the sites/activities framework is not yet live for BioCCS).
- Because in-situ mineralisation is not in the list of storage modules with configured monitoring submissions, the storage-site monitoring plan for it may need Isometric to configure it; confirm with the ROM before relying on API monitoring submissions.
- Whether a BioCCS project gets the Feedstock tab (doc says "BiCRS projects using the Biomass Feedstock Accounting module"; BioCCS v1.5 also uses that module, v1.4) is unclear; ask the ROM.

### Gaps
- No public doc lists the default LCA template components for BioCCS + in-situ mineralisation.
- No public Certify doc specific to BioCCS storage-site validation requirements for the in-situ module.

## 5. What does Preview mode allow and restrict, and how does a supplier exit it?

### Takeaway
Any supplier can self-register a free Certify account; it opens in Preview ("trial") mode. Preview allows creating draft projects, seeing and exporting the full PDD requirement list, building an LCA, trying a demo project and using the MRV APIs; Registry credit-management features (deliver, retire, transfer, etc.) are disabled. Exit is by signing the Supplier Agreement with the Partnerships team, which "unlock[s] all features".

## 6. Timing rules: crediting period, start-date limits, verification/issuance frequency, ex-post issuance, buffer pool

### Takeaway
BioCCS crediting period is 15 years (Standard v2.0+), renewable without limit via updated PDD and re-validation. The requested crediting-period start can be no earlier than 5 years before initial registry submission. Issuance is strictly ex-post after VVB verification; verification is at least annual (more often allowed; Isometric markets monthly), and >12 months without verification forces a PDD update and re-validation. For in-situ mineralisation credits are earned on injection and isolation; buffer contribution is typically 1% (Very Low risk), 0% if the site is under a recognised equivalent regulatory regime (e.g. EU CCS Directive).

### Cited Findings
- Crediting period: general maximum 10 years unless a protocol says otherwise; "Biogenic CCS and Direct Air Capture Protocols have 15-year periods"; renewal requires an updated PDD and re-Validation, no limit on renewals while compliant (Standard §3.4). — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- v2.0 (2 Apr 2026): default crediting periods raised from five to 10 years, 15 years for DAC and Bio-CCS; buffer pool removed for open-system pathways and for "projects under robust regulatory schemes that already require equivalent protections", e.g. the EU CCS Directive; the protocol version a supplier registers against is locked for the crediting period. — [Isometric certifies version 2.0 of the Standard](https://isometric.com/writing-articles/isometric-certifies-version-2-0-of-the-standard); Standard changelog also records "Contributions reduced from 2% to 1% for 'Very Low Risk' projects" — [Standard changelog](https://registry.isometric.com/standard/2.2/changelog)
- Start date: "The requested Crediting Period Start Date can be no earlier than 5 years before the initial Registry submission"; GHG statements can cover activity from the Start Date onward (§3.4). Projects must use the latest certified Protocol at application (§3.3). — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- Frequency: VVB verification "annual or more frequent, per the Protocol, or at the proponent's request"; a project must update its PDD and be re-validated if it has no Verification for more than 12 months since the last one (§3.4). "Isometric does not issue ex-ante Certificates" (§3.4). — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- Buffer pool sizes by Risk of Reversal (§5.6.2): No observable risk 0%; Very low 1%; Low 5%; Medium 7%; High 10–20%. Exempt if "No observable risk", under a recognised equivalent regulatory scheme, or in the insurance pilot. Buffer certificates cannot be transferred or delivered during the Crediting Period. Reversals must be reported within one business day of identification (no later than three); buffer certificates equal to the reversal are cancelled; avoidable reversals must be reimbursed; "Ceasing monitoring is treated as an avoidable reversal". — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- In-situ module: "Crediting will occur on injection into the reservoir and isolation from the atmosphere"; "typically categorized as Very Low Risk, resulting in a 1% buffer pool"; reassessed at crediting-period renewal or when new science emerges. — [In-situ Mineralization Module v1.2](https://registry.isometric.com/module/in-situ-mineralization)
- BioCCS Appendix 4: geologic storage typically "Very Low Risk Level" = 1% buffer; reversal risk reassessed at each crediting-period renewal, on monitoring-identified risk, or after a reversal. — [BioCCS Protocol v1.5](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/biogenic-capture-and-storage)
- Credits split: verified statements produce supplier credits plus a buffer-pool portion ("A portion of credits will also be split and allocated to a buffer pool"); each credit unit = 1 kg CO₂e; serial format e.g. `ISO-1-ACME-USA-0A1B-2023-1395450-1401153`; supplier and buffer credits are separate batches. — [GHG statements](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/ghg-statement.md); [Key Registry Concepts](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/registry/key-registry-concepts.md)
- Version adoption: projects need not adopt new minor/major protocol versions until crediting-period renewal; may adopt earlier (ROM performs the upgrade; changes documented in the GHG statement report; usually no new site visit); patch versions apply automatically. — [Protocol versioning](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/registry/protocol-versioning.md); [Protocol versions and upgrades FAQs](https://docs.isometric.com/support/working-with-isometric/protocol-versions-and-upgrades.md)
- Data gaps in BioCCS must stay under 5% of data used for removals and emissions in a reporting period; records kept ≥5 years after the end of the monitoring period. — [BioCCS Protocol v1.5](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/biogenic-capture-and-storage/1.5)

### Inferences
- First-issuance date can include removals back to the validated start date (≤5 years before submission), so injections made before onboarding can be credited if the data meets protocol requirements.
- A non-EU storage site would most likely carry the 1% buffer unless Isometric accepts the host-country regime as "equivalent".

### Gaps
- No explicit maximum reporting-period length beyond the 12-month re-validation trigger was found in BioCCS v1.5's visible text.

## 7. Which Isometric protocols and modules apply to this project type, and what are their current versions?

### Takeaway
Protocol: **Biogenic Carbon Capture and Storage (BioCCS) v1.5** (not DAC, not BiCRS). Its v1.5 module set includes Biomass Feedstock Accounting v1.4, GHG Accounting v1.1, Energy Use Accounting v1.3, Shared CO₂ Transport Infrastructure v1.0, and storage options including **In-Situ Mineralization v1.2**. Material eligibility risk: BioCCS lists point sources such as biomass power, CHP, bio-hydrogen, biogas and energy-from-waste and does not mention cement or kilns; the creditable biogenic fraction of mixed flue gas must be measured (radiocarbon or mass balance), fossil feedstocks >5% by mass need justification, and limestone calcination CO₂ is treated as non-biogenic.

### Cited Findings
- BioCCS v1.5 module list: Biomass Feedstock Accounting Module v1.4; GHG Accounting Module v1.1; Energy Use Accounting Module v1.3; Shared CO₂ Transport Infrastructure Module v1.0; storage: Saline Aquifer v1.2, Depleted Hydrocarbon Reservoirs v1.1, In-Situ Mineralization v1.2, Ex-Situ Mineralization in Closed Engineered Systems v1.1 (with Built Materials storage v1.1), Enhanced Weathering in Closed Engineered Systems v1.0 (with DIC storage in oceans v1.0). — [BioCCS Protocol v1.5](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/biogenic-capture-and-storage)
- Version history: v1.3 added energy-from-waste, inseparable biogenic/fossil feedstocks, fossil co-firing for start-up/shutdown/flame support, centralised GHG Accounting Module, retrofit rules, ETS considerations; v1.4 aligned buffer pools and crediting periods with Standard v2.0; v1.5 added the Shared CO₂ Transport Infrastructure Module, batch-basis CO₂ measurement guidance and behind-the-meter energy clarification. — [BioCCS v1.5 changelog](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/biogenic-capture-and-storage/1.5/changelog)
- Scope: capture of biogenic CO₂ "at a point-source, resulting from processing of an eligible biomass" feedstock with >1,000-year storage via a CO₂ storage module; examples listed (non-exhaustive): biomass power plants, biomass CHP, bio-hydrogen, biogas plants, energy-from-waste. Fossil-origin CO₂ not eligible. "Projects where more than 5% of feedstocks by mass are fossil fuels must provide a justification"; Isometric may decline to credit a reporting period otherwise (R-YJJC-0). Mixed-fossil flue gas: biogenic fraction F_B by radiocarbon (Method A) or mass balance (Method B). The text cites "emissions of CO2 from calcination of limestone" (§8.5.6.3) and "Cryogenic processes for CO2 purification or liquefaction" (§8.5.6.2) in the emissions accounting. Keyword checks for "cement", "kiln", "clinker", "truck", "road" found no hits in the visible text. — [BioCCS Protocol v1.5](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/biogenic-capture-and-storage/1.5)
- Approved cap-and-trade schemes for ETS interaction: only EU ETS and UK ETS (Appendix 3). — [BioCCS Protocol v1.5](https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/biogenic-capture-and-storage)
- In-situ module v1.2 eligibility: onshore mafic/ultramafic formations (e.g. basalts, ophiolites); dissolved or supercritical injection; current well permit naming CO₂ as an acceptable injectant (US: Class VI); "Sites outside approved permitting regimes need a monitoring plan signed off by a licensed geoscientist" (report covering site characterisation, AOR and reservoir modelling with uncertainty, risk register, SRMS maturity opinion); no storage where enhanced hydrocarbon recovery could affect the reservoir; mass flow meters 2% full-scale accuracy, calibrated at least annually. Post-injection: periodic mineralisation/plume assessment at 15 years (USA) or 20 years (EU) or equivalent, default ≥50 years if the regulator sets none, unless mineralisation is proven sooner. — [In-situ Mineralization Module v1.2](https://registry.isometric.com/module/in-situ-mineralization)
- Spring 2026 release (12 May 2026): In-situ Mineralization 1.2 (and Saline Aquifer 1.2, etc.) lets suppliers "submit approved government permits as evidence of compliance", with each module listing which permitting schemes are sufficient. — [Spring 2026 update](https://isometric.com/writing-articles/spring-2026-update)
- A CRCF-specific BioCCS requirements module exists (`bio-ccs-requirements-crcf/1.0`). — [registry.isometric.com/module/bio-ccs-requirements-crcf/1.0](https://registry.isometric.com/module/bio-ccs-requirements-crcf/1.0)
- Truck transport is modelled in the LCA with the "Transport emissions" blueprint (mass × distance × factor) or "Fuel usage by volume" when fuel is metered. — [Build an LCA](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/lca.md)

### Inferences
- There is no separate road-transport module; truck haulage of liquefied CO₂ is handled inside the GHG Accounting / Energy Use modules and LCA components. The Shared CO₂ Transport Infrastructure Module concerns allocating emissions of shared infrastructure, which may or may not apply.
- Eligibility of a cement host is the largest open risk: it should be the first question for the optional eligibility assessment (biogenic share of kiln fuel, F_B measurement method, exclusion of calcination CO₂, fossil >5% justification).
- If the host country's permitting regime is not on the module's "approved" list, a licensed-geoscientist-signed monitoring plan becomes a PDD prerequisite.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm BioCCS v1.5 certification date or whether any cement-kiln BioCCS project has been accepted.
- The in-situ module's list of approved permitting regimes was not extracted.

## 8. What is publicly disclosed, and when?

### Takeaway
Disclosure is staged: supplier listing after the Supplier Agreement (can be deferred); project page + redacted PDD at public comment / "Under validation"; validation report on the registry page; at issuance, the calculation view and datapoints for each GHG entry, public source files, and filenames of non-public sources. Commercially sensitive content can be redacted, but baselines and critical assumptions cannot be withheld.

### Cited Findings
- New project visible only to supplier and Isometric's tech team; on verifier assignment the verifier sees title, supplier, protocol; "When a project enters the public comment period, it will be listed on the Isometric registry. Published data includes the project name, location, description, process diagram, protocol and Project Design Document". Draft PDD/LCA shared with the verifier after pre-screen; redacted publication version agreed between Isometric and supplier. GHG entry data goes to the verifier on statement submission; "On credit issuance, the calculation view and datapoints supporting an individual GHG entry are published"; public source files downloadable; non-public sources listed by filename only. — [Data visibility](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/data-visibility.md)
- Redactions: "your Registry Operations Manager will agree any required redactions of sections containing commercially sensitive or personal information, before publishing a copy". — [Project design](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/certify/project-design.md)
- Buyer listings: a project "is listed automatically when it reaches Under validation, which is also when it gets a public registry page"; forecast, price and availability can be hidden; projects can be listed earlier (draft or placeholder); cancelled or unsuccessfully validated projects are delisted. Listings (buyer-only, not public) show description, location, protocol and validation progress from Certify. — [Manage project listings](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/registry/manage-project-listings.md); [Project listings](https://docs.isometric.com/user-guides/registry/project-listings.md)
- Standard: all data, calculations and evidence behind certificates public; "Sensitive business information, such as locations, names, and proprietary processes, can be withheld... Baselines and critical underlying assumptions cannot be withheld" (§3.2); monitoring reports public (§2.5.7.2); validation, verification and surveillance reports available per project; ownership history public per certificate. — [Isometric Standard v2.2](https://registry.isometric.com/standard)
- VVB statements and reports made public "subject to commercial confidentiality constraints". — [VVB Policy v1.2](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KOmLuhU6yzCYYtUWPyQCw4aByEEJ7Q1-/view)
- Published PDD and reports are retrievable via `GET /registry/v0/projects/{id}/documents`. — [Project Documents API](https://docs.isometric.com/api-reference/registry/project-documents.md)

### Inferences
- Mark commercially sensitive Certify sources (capture-unit design, host-site contracts, pricing) as non-public before the first statement; only filenames will surface.

### Gaps
- None material.
