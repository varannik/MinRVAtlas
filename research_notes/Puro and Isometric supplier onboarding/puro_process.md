# Puro.earth supplier journey: first contact to first CORC issuance (GSC facility, as of November 2026)

Scope: the process, gates, fees, tools and timing rules for a CO2 Removal Supplier ("Operator" in the 2026 documents) taking a Geologically Stored Carbon (GSC) production facility from first contact to first CORC issuance on Puro.earth. The facility in question captures biogenic CO2 at a cement plant, liquefies and transports it, and stores it by in-situ mineralisation in ultramafic rock. The detailed per-section contents of the GSC audit package are covered elsewhere and appear here only at a high level.

Sources were read on 14 Nov 2026. The primary PDFs were downloaded from Puro's Document Library (hubspotusercontent CDN) and their text extracted. Abbreviations used below:
- CP = Certification Procedures v1.2
- GR = General Rules v4.4
- GSC = GSC methodology Edition 2024 v5
- CC = Common Criteria v1.0
- AAR = Additionality Assessment Requirements v2.1
- PA = Platform Agreement
- PCN = Project Concept Note
- PAD = Project Activity Description
- MP = Monitoring Period
- VVB = Validation and Verification Body

## 1. What are the stages of the supplier journey, and what are the entry and exit gates of each?

### Takeaway
Puro's public "certification journey" has eight stages: Initial Qualification → Platform Agreement + KYC → Facility Registration → optional Preliminary Assessment → Audit Preparation (Puro pre-review) → VVB Facility Audit (validation) → VVB Output Audit (verification) → CORC Issuance. Since 28 May 2026 the binding gates sit in a new layer of documents that the local spec does not mention: the program-agnostic **Puro.earth Certification Procedures (v1.2, 30 Sep 2026)**, the **Puro Program Annex v1.0** and the **Common Criteria v1.0**. These sit alongside **General Rules v4.4 (7 May 2026)**, which is still published as the "latest" Puro Standard rulebook. The two layers disagree on several timing rules (see Inferences). A new GSC facility should get Puro's written confirmation of which rule set governs it.

### Cited Findings

#### Version map (which documents apply now)

Each version takes effect on its issue date. It is "a generic normative document applicable by default to all Programs operated by Puro.earth", with program-specific changes set out in Annexes (s.1.2, 1.4, Document History). — [Certification Procedures v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)

  - The Common Criteria "does not govern operator obligations directly; obligations arise through methodology document incorporation".
  - Source: [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)

Operators must use the latest published templates (4.3.3) and submit a "Template Version Declaration" with the application (5.1.3). — same

#### Stage-by-stage gates
- **Public 8-stage journey (Puro certification page):**
  1. **Initial qualification**: Puro assesses readiness and eligibility "against a Puro Standard, CRCF or CCS+ methodology", starting from the "Certify Credits form".
  2. **Platform Agreement Signature**: KYC verification plus "the annual €1,400 Account Holder fee", which gives access to the Puro Registry.
  3. **Facility Registration** in the Puro Registry.
  4. **Preliminary Assessment** (optional).
  5. **Audit Preparation**: the supplier submits the Audit Document Package, and Puro reviews it "before a VVB is engaged".
  6. **VVB Facility Audit**: "valid up to five years".
  7. **VVB Output Audit**: on-demand for Issuance Plus suppliers.
  8. **CORC Issuances**.
  - Source: [Puro: Carbon credit certification](https://puro.earth/cdr-infrastructure/carbon-credit-certification/)
- The supplier FAQ gives a 7-step version: Initial Qualification (often via the questionnaire at connect.puro.earth/newsupplieroutreach) → Platform Agreement (with "KYC checks run in parallel"; on completion the supplier becomes an Account Holder with MyPuro access) → Facility Registration in MyPuro (a "unique registry identification number" is assigned) → Audit Preparation → optional Preliminary Assessment (success allows listing as a "Future Facility") → Third-Party Facility Audit (desktop review, on-site visit, interviews) → Output Audits & CORC Issuance "at least annually". — [Puro CDR supplier FAQs](https://puro.earth/insights/puro-earth-faqs/cdr-supplier-faqs/)
- **Gate 1, Initial Qualification.** The Operator completes a **Project Concept Note (PCN)** containing:
  - (A) Operator entity and contacts
  - (B) a non-technical summary, including the methodology and credit generation estimates
  - (C) an implementation timeline, including plans for stakeholder engagement and PAD development
  - (D) previous certification attempts with another scheme, including by a legal predecessor
  - (E) a declaration that the activity is not certified elsewhere, or proof of deregistration
  - Puro decides whether the Operator can proceed. Grounds to stop: "absence of an applicable approved Methodology, Project Activities expected to generate negligible annual credit volumes, and/or previous audit failures with another certification scheme" (CP 4.1.1-4.1.4).
  - Source: [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Gate 2, KYC then Platform Agreement.** KYC checks follow qualification (4.2.1). "If the KYC checks are satisfactory, the Operator shall sign a Platform Agreement committing to undergoing certification … and thereby agrees to the fee schedule" (4.2.2). On request, the activity is then listed publicly in the Registry as **"Under Development"** with the PCN information (4.2.4; Table 4). — same
- **Gate 3, Facility registration.** GR 4.4 makes "Submitted the Production Facility registration" a precondition of the Facility Audit (GR 2.2.4.1 iii). — [General Rules v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **Gate 4, Application for certification / audit package.** The Operator submits, in English: the PAD, the Monitoring Plan, the "information to be made public in the Puro Registry", and a Monitoring Report "only if Validation is combined with Verification" (CP 5.1.2), plus the Template Version Declaration (5.1.3). GR 4.4 frames the same gate as audit documentation "determined complete by the Issuing Body" (GR 2.2.4.1 iv). — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf); [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **Gate 5, Puro completeness review and VVB allocation.** Puro reviews whether the documentation is "complete and sufficient to attempt a Validation and/or Verification audit". If it is, "Puro.earth shall allocate a VVB … and the Operator shall confirm acceptance of the allocated VVB". If it is not, Puro gives reasons and the Operator revises and resubmits. "If after multiple revision cycles" the application is still insufficient, Puro may deregister the activity (CP 5.2.1-5.2.4). Registry status changes to **"Under Validation"** once the completeness check is done and the audit is scheduled (Table 4). — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Gate 6, Validation (facility audit).**
  - Conducted at reasonable assurance under ISO 14064-3:2019 (5.3.1-5.3.2).
  - The VVB may issue clarification requests (5.3.14) and submits a Validation Audit Report (5.3.15).
  - Puro runs an internal review (5.6.1). "New client", "New methodology" and "New VVB" always trigger a **full review** (8.2.5).
  - Puro accepts the report only once all clarification requests are closed (5.6.2).
  - Puro then publishes the Validation Audit Report, PAD and Monitoring Plan, and the status becomes **"Validated"** (the "validation date") (5.6.3).
  - Source: same
- **Gate 7, Verification (output audit) and issuance.**
  - The VVB's independent reviewer signs off and the Verification Audit Report goes to Puro's Certification Office for internal review (5.4.10-5.4.11).
  - After a successful review the Monitoring Report is published and the status becomes **"Verified"** (5.6.4).
  - "Credits are issued only after a successful review by the Puro.earth of the associated Verification Audit Report" (9.2.2), into the Operator's registry account (9.2.8), as integers with any remainder carried forward (9.2.9).
  - Source: same
- GR 4.4 calls these phases **Design Validation** (Production Facility Audit by the VVB, then Production Facility Review by Puro) and **Performance Verification** (Output Audit, then Output Review) (GR 2.2-2.3). — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **Registry statuses (CP Table 4):**
  - Under Development: PA signed and PCN submitted
  - Under Validation: completeness check done and audit scheduled
  - Validated: successful Puro review of the Validation Report
  - Verified: successful Puro review of the Verification Report
  - Rejected: unsuccessful review, open non-conformities, or double registration
  - Suspended: non-conformities not resolved in time, "invoices unpaid", or a reversal notification
  - Expired: end of crediting period
  - Terminated: cancelled voluntarily by the Operator
  - Withdrawn: cancelled by Puro
  - Source: [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Non-conformity gates (CP s.6).** These are new relative to the GR "30 days, one resubmission" rule.
  - A **critical** non-conformity at validation means the project is Rejected, and it can re-apply only after 12 months. At verification it means Withdrawn.
  - A **major** non-conformity at validation means Rejected (re-apply after 12 months, within the validation deadlines). At verification the Certificate of Compliance is Suspended. CARs/FARs must be implemented within "max. 3 months". If remediation is not implemented within 90 days, the activity is withdrawn.
  - A **minor** non-conformity must be resolved within 12 months or before the next verification, whichever is earlier, or the VVB may waive it to the next audit.
  - Fraud or "failure to pay invoices on time" can lead to immediate suspension or withdrawal (6.1.6).
  - Source: same
- GR 4.4 still states the older rule: after an unsuccessful Facility or Output Review, the supplier has "a reasonable opportunity to address the identified non-conformity issues within 30 days and submit once new/revised documentation for a re-review" (GR 2.2.5.3, 2.3.3.3). — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **Preliminary Assessment** (optional) is aimed at "pre-operational suppliers in project development". It provides eligibility confirmation, early identification of gaps, and "listing in Puro.earth's facility listings" for investor visibility. No fee is published. — [Puro: Preliminary Assessment](https://puro.earth/preliminary-assessment)
- dMRV Connect guidance: a new facility typically starts with a Preliminary Assessment monitoring period. "Preliminary Assessments and Facility Audits share documents within the same monitoring period", but the correct framework type must be submitted in the Audit Package. For a combined facility and output audit, apply both the Facility Audit and the Output Audit frameworks. — [dMRV Connect: Applying frameworks](https://docs.api.puro.earth/dmrv/guides/setting-up-monitoring-periods/applying-frameworks)

### Inferences
- For a first-of-kind GSC mineralisation facility, the practical critical path runs: Initial Qualification (jurisdiction eligibility under GSC 3.2.11 is the most likely early blocker, see Q7) → KYC/PA, which **locks GSC 2024 v5** unless a later version is elected before validation → registration → optional Preliminary Assessment while pre-operational → combined Validation + first Verification once injection has started. GSC 2.2.1(b) requires storage sites to be operational at the Facility Audit, so a stand-alone validation before injection seems unlikely to be accepted for GSC.
- The PA signature date matters strategically. It fixes the methodology version, starts the €1,400 annual fee clock, and (per CP Table 4) allows the "Under Development" listing.

#### Where the local spec (docs/puro-dmrv-integration-spec.md, compiled Sep 2026) is wrong or out of date
1. **Missing rule layer.** The spec cites only GR v4.4. It does not mention the Certification Procedures (v1.0 May 2026, now v1.2 30 Sep 2026), the Puro Program Annex, the Common Criteria or the Clarifications (06 Nov 2026), which now define the gates, non-conformity handling and registry statuses.
2. **Crediting period.** The spec says "5 years by default". For GSC it is **15 years**, starting on the first date of the first MP and renewable twice (GSC 2.2.2). CP 3.2.1 sets a 10-year default only where a methodology is silent. The marketing page's "valid up to five years" is generic and does not apply to GSC.
3. **Validation deadline.** The spec says 3 years from the Commitment Date (GR 2.2.3). CP 5.3.9 allows **5 years** for activities that need "development and permitting of geological CO2 storage infrastructure, specifically such as BioCCS and DACCS". This conflicts with GR 4.4 for the Puro Program and needs confirming with Puro.
4. **Failure handling.** The spec says "30-day window and one resubmission". That is still in GR 4.4, but CP s.6 adds a graded critical/major/minor regime (major: CARs within 3 months, withdrawal at 90 days; rejected projects re-apply after 12 months).
5. **First period length.** The spec says an Output Report may cover up to 18 months (GR 2.4.2). CP 5.4.5 says "The first Verification Audit may cover a period of up to 24 (twenty-four) months." These conflict.
6. **Crediting-period start.** GR 2.4.1 and GSC 2.2.2 start it at the first date of the first MP. CP 3.2.2 says it "shall begin on the Project Start Date". These conflict.
7. **Site visits.** The spec says the output audit "includes a desk study and a site visit". Clarification #028 (21 Jul 2026) lets the auditor decide that no site visit is needed for an Output Audit, especially under Issuance Plus.
8. **VVB assignment.** The spec says it is "opaque, server-side". Puro still allocates the VVB, but CP 5.2.2 now requires the Operator to "confirm acceptance of the allocated VVB".
9. **Reversal remedy.** The spec says that as of v4.4, EU/UK emission allowances can compensate. GR 6.7.5.4 / CP 9.10.7 actually say that in EEA/UK jurisdictions where the storage operator must by law surrender allowances, **Puro executes no other remedial action**. The supplier does not deposit allowances to Puro. CP 9.10.12 also allows a future program-level buffer pool for Tier III activities.
10. **dMRV docs URL.** The spec's `https://docs.api.puro.earth/dmrv/` returned 404 on 14 Nov 2026. The live entry point is `https://docs.api.puro.earth/dmrv/overview`.

### Gaps
- No public document explains how GR 4.4 and CP v1.2 rank against each other for a Puro Program facility on the points where they conflict (validation deadline, crediting-period start, first-period length). The Annex says it prevails over CP and CC and lists GR as a Puro Program normative document, but GR is not formally an "Annex". This needs Puro's written confirmation (CP 4.3.4 provides for written confirmations).
- The Compatibility Matrix (CP 4.3.4) did not appear in the Document Library listing as of 14 Nov 2026.
- The V&V Requirements v1.3 PDF link returned HTML, not a PDF, so its content (for example VVB rotation and site-visit rules) was not read.

## 2. What does KYC / account-holder onboarding require, and what agreements are signed?

### Takeaway
Puro runs risk-based **KYB** on the company and **KYC** on its authorised representative before the Platform Agreement is signed. Signing the Platform Agreement makes the supplier an Account Holder, opens a Puro Registry account automatically (on payment of fees), commits it to certification and to the fee schedule, and fixes the methodology version. I found no separate public "Supplier Agreement" or "Registry Account Terms". The registry terms and conditions are "defined in the Platform Agreement", and its text is not public.

### Cited Findings
- KYB/KYC/AML Policies **v1.2 (approved 28 Apr 2026)**. Earlier versions: v1.0 25 Mar 2024; v1.1 17 Sep 2025, which introduced the "risk exposure–based verification approach". Puro "may choose to work with an external partner to deliver these services". — [Puro KYB/KYC/AML Policies v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Puro%20Earth%20KYC-AML%20Policies%20V1.2.pdf)
- **KYB steps:**
  - **Registration**: "name, registered address, company identification number, and other relevant contact details", plus "name and email of the person who will act as the authorized representative".
  - **Verification**: "copy of the company registration certificate, register of shareholders and other relevant documents depending on the level of risk exposure". Puro may also ask for "the annual report, financial statements, environmental impact assessment, or third-party certifications".
  - **Screening**: sanctions, restrictions and adverse media, using for example "the European Union's sanctions list, the United Nations' consolidated list, or the World-Check database".
  - **Approval**: formal approval is required "before it can start issuing and managing CORCs".
  - **Monitoring**: ongoing checks against sanctions lists and PEP lists. A hit leads to "contract termination procedure".
  - Source: same
- **Risk-based tiers.** A simplified KYB applies to "lower-risk client categories", meaning entities "subject to statutory reporting, continuous disclosure, and regulatory oversight, with ownership structures that are transparent and verifiable". Puro may escalate to full KYB or KYC where screening shows money laundering or terrorist financing risk, sanctions exposure, adverse media, or "complex, opaque" ownership. — same
- **KYC on the individual.** Puro collects the "name, email address, position of the individual designated as the client's authorized representative, as well as proof of the individual's authorized signatory rights". Puro "has opted to omit requirement of KYC for lower-risk client categories". — same
- Clients must "promptly notify of any changes". Puro may suspend or terminate accounts for non-compliance or false information (s.4). Puro may repeat KYC at any time on reasonable grounds (CP 4.2.5). — same; [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Account opening conditions (CP 9.1.2):** pass KYC; "agree to the Terms and Conditions as defined in the Platform Agreement"; "pay the annual fee for each period for which it wishes the Account to remain open". For Project Operators, "a Registry account will be opened automatically upon signature of the Platform Agreement and payment of relevant fees" (9.1.3). — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **What the Platform Agreement commits the supplier to:**
  - certification of the Project Activity under the relevant program(s) and the fee schedule (CP 4.2.2)
  - the methodology version fixed at signature (CP 4.2.3)
  - service fees for all registry transactions "as defined in the Platform Agreement" (CP 9.1.1; GR 3.1.1)
  - reporting "all such sales immediately to the Registry Operator" and paying the service fee when trading in any venue (GR 4.1.1)
  - on termination, completing "any Audits still due" and paying "any fees still due" (CP 9.7.3; GR 3.1.5)
  - Puro has the right to suspend accounts for missed reporting timelines or breach of the Terms (CP 9.1.4)
  - Sources: [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf); [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- "All System participants need to be registered Account Holders of the System and need to have signed the Platform Agreement. The Issuing Body shall perform standard know your customer checks for each Account Holder" (GR 1.1.4). An Account Holder is a "Legal entity who has signed a Platform Agreement" (GR Definitions). — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- The annual fee is "1400€ per 12-month period, starting from the date of signing the Platform Agreement". Opening, closing or reactivating accounts costs nothing extra. — [Puro Fees](https://puro.earth/certified-carbon-credits/fees/)
- **GSC-specific corporate evidence:** a "certified trade registry extract or similar official document stating that it is validly existing and in compliance with the legislation of the host jurisdiction" for the supplier (GSC 3.3.1) **and for every external operator** (3.3.5). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- **Signatory attestation:** "The CO2 Removal Supplier shall attest to the accuracy of the information provided by its signature. The signatory shall be an individual with legal signing authority within the CO2 Removal Supplier" (GR 2.2.4.3). — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)

### Inferences
- A supplier that is an unlisted, newly formed CDR company, possibly with a parent or joint-venture structure, should expect **full KYB**: shareholder register, ultimate beneficial ownership, and possibly financial statements. The simplified tier is aimed at regulated or listed entities.
- The policy does not use the term "beneficial ownership". In practice, the "register of shareholders" and the escalation trigger for ownership structures that "cannot be sufficiently verified" do that job.
- The same person should be lined up as KYC'd authorised representative, PA signatory and GR 2.2.4.3 attestation signatory, to avoid repeating KYC.

### Gaps
- The Platform Agreement text and any General Terms and Conditions are not published, so the governing law, liability caps, termination notice, invoicing and payment terms, and IP/data licence terms could not be checked.
- No public list of KYB documents per risk tier, and no turnaround time for KYB/KYC.
- I found no evidence of a separate "Supplier Agreement" or "Registry Account Terms" document under those names.

## 3. What information does facility registration ask for?

### Takeaway
GR 4.4 lists nine registration data items. CP v1.2 adds the PCN fields and the public registry fields. GSC adds the facility definition (capture + logistics + storage sites), the CO2 ownership chain and external-operator contracts. The exact MyPuro form fields are not public.

### Cited Findings
- **GR 3.1.2.1 registration details:**
  - (i) the supplier registering the facility
  - (ii) the supplier's "right to represent the Production Facility and to be the entity that receives the CORCs"
  - (iii) the registry account associated with the facility
  - (iv) the facility name
  - (v) its location
  - (vi) the host country and sector
  - (vii) the applicable methodology
  - (viii) methodology-specific information
  - (ix) "Proof of ownership of the Production Facility, and/or legal rights to the CORCs"
  - Registration is "initiated in the Registry by the CO2 Removal Supplier" (3.1.2).
  - Source: [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- The minimum project description must open with "Production Facility name, location, Methodology, CO2 Removal Supplier" (GR 2.2.4.2 i). — same
- **Ownership evidence (CP 4.4):** the Operator must hold "the exclusive legal right to the credits … for the duration of the Crediting Period, including the authority to access the Registry and to receive hold, transfer, or retire such credits". Acceptable evidence includes contracts, land-use or tenure documents, licences, concessions or permits, partnership or JV agreements, and declarations that no competing claims exist. A transfer of project ownership requires notice, legal documentation and KYC of the new entity. Issuance is suspended until the transfer completes, and also during ownership disputes (4.4.4-4.4.7). — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Public registry fields (CP 9.9.2):**
  - activity name and unique ID
  - Operator
  - program and methodology
  - status
  - crediting period start and end dates
  - Certificate of Compliance status
  - credits issued, transferred, cancelled and retired
  - monitoring and verification history including audit reports
  - methodology transitions
  - CORC attributes also include the facility identifier, name and location, the host country and sector, and the MP dates (CP 9.2.6; GR 3.2.4.1).
  - Source: same
- The FAQ says facility registration happens in MyPuro and the facility "receives a unique registry identification number". — [Puro CDR supplier FAQs](https://puro.earth/insights/puro-earth-faqs/cdr-supplier-faqs/)
- **GSC facility definition:**
  - The Production Facility is "the ensemble of physical assets necessary to perform the end-to-end activities … one or several capture sites, a logistic chain for carbon dioxide transport, and one or several storage sites".
  - Capture sites must be similar in nature (feedstock, capture technology, supplier), in the same jurisdiction, and operational at the Facility Audit. Storage sites must be operational at the Facility Audit.
  - Any change in the facility definition during the crediting period requires an update of the Facility Audit (GSC 2.2.1).
  - The **CO2 Removal Supplier** is "the party contractually authorized to represent the participants necessary to perform the end-to-end activities", for example the capture operator, the capture owner, or the owner of the captured CO2. It "does not need to be the operator of the process creating the CO2" (2.1.1). The cement plant operator therefore need not be the supplier.
  - Source: [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- **GSC owner vs operator evidence:**
  - The supplier keeps sole rights to the removal (3.3.2a) and sole ownership of the stored CO2, except where local regulation requires a transfer or there is a mutual-agreement transfer to the storage operator (3.3.2b).
  - Permits for all installations within the boundary (3.3.3).
  - A contractual split of responsibilities with external operators covering monitoring, leakage/reversal response, and closure and post-closure costs (3.3.4).
  - Contracts showing permanent storage, proof of CO2 ownership, attestations of no claim, and a **right to audit** external operators' documents and equipment (3.3.5).
  - Source: same

### Inferences
- For a cement-capture → liquefaction → transport → mineralisation chain, register **one Production Facility** covering the capture site, the logistics chain and the storage site. If the cement plant owner, the capture operator, the transporter and the storage/injection operator are different legal entities, the registration must already be backed by the GSC 3.3.4/3.3.5 contract set and no-claim attestations.

### Gaps
- The exact MyPuro registration form fields (for example capacity, start-of-operations date, coordinates, owner vs operator) are not public. The MyPuro product page lists none. — [MyPuro](https://puro.earth/products/mypuro/)

### Takeaway
**Puro allocates the VVB, and the Operator confirms acceptance. Puro pays the VVB.** The supplier pays:

- a per-CORC service fee tiered on trailing 364-day volume (€10.00 down to €0.40), invoiced at trade or retirement, with a 5% discount for paying at issuance; or a **flat €12,000 per audit** if at or below 1,000 CORCs

## 5. How long does the Production Facility Audit take, how long is it valid, and when is it renewed? Design Validation vs facility audit.

### Takeaway
"Design Validation" (GR 4.4), or "Validation / Certification" (CP v1.2), is the umbrella phase: **VVB Production Facility Audit + Puro Production Facility Review**. The audit is the VVB's part. For GSC the validation result holds for the **15-year crediting period**. Puro's marketing "up to five years" is the generic default. Renewal needs a new validation against the latest methodology, with the package submitted at least 90 days before expiry. Deadline: validation within 3 years of the Commitment Date under GR 4.4 / AAR 2.1, or 5 years for geological-storage projects under CP 5.3.9. No public figure exists for the audit's duration.

### Cited Findings
- "Design validation … involves a Production Facility Audit by an independent 3rd party auditor, followed by a Production Facility Review by the Issuing Body". It "shall be completed within three (3) years of the Commitment Date" (GR 2.2.1, 2.2.3). — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **Commitment Date:** "the calendar date on which the CO2 removal Supplier … committed to implementing the CO2 Removal activity (e.g., the date when contracts for the purchase or installation of equipment … were signed)". Without capex, it is the date of first physical actions (GR Definitions). — same
- AAR v2.1 (approved 18 Jun 2026) repeats the 3-year rule and lists exceptions:
  - (a) transfer from a program whose audit is deemed equivalent
  - (b) equipment installed but unused, or used at less than 10% of capacity
  - (c) emergencies (natural disaster, pandemic)
  - Additionality is audited at the first facility audit and at each crediting-period renewal (2.1.1-2.1.3).
  - Source: [AAR v2.1](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Standard%20Additionality%20Assessment%20Requirements%20v2.1.pdf)
- CP v1.2 5.3.8-5.3.9: validation within 3 years of the Commitment Date, except that "Project Activities which require a significantly longer development time due to the development and permitting of geological CO2 storage infrastructure, specifically such as BioCCS and DACCS activities, shall complete Validation within five (5) years of the Commitment Date, unless a shorter or different timeline is specified in the applicable methodology document or Program Annex." — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **GSC crediting period:** "15 years starting from the first date of the first monitoring period … can be renewed twice by successfully undergoing a new Production Facility Audit" (GSC 2.2.2). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- GR 2.4.1 default: 5 years unless the methodology says otherwise, renewable twice. Renewal requires re-evaluating everything in the facility audit, "explicitly" including baseline reassessment. — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **CP renewal package (5.7):** a Renewal Assessment (CC 1.4), the final Monitoring Report, the current Monitoring Plan, and updated PAD annexes. It must be submitted "no later than 90 days before the end of the expiring crediting period". Puro reviews it before a VVB is scheduled. The VVB then runs a validation of the Renewal Assessment and a verification of the final Monitoring Report. — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Mid-period re-validation** against a revised methodology is possible if at least 18 months remain in the crediting period (CP 3.2.7; GR 2.3.5.2). Under GR, the update is combined with the next Output Audit and the crediting-period dates stay unchanged (2.3.5.1, 2.3.5.4). — same; [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **Material changes** require an updated PAD/MP and re-validation. Puro may suspend the activity until the new validation completes (CP 5.8). GR 3.1.3.1 likewise allows suspension and a new Facility Audit. — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- The **minimum Facility Audit documentation** is a project description covering 11 items (GR 2.2.4.2): name, location, methodology and supplier; non-technical description; technical description; net removal quantification with uncertainty; reversal risk estimation and monitoring; conformity to the methodology; monitoring and reporting plan; Baseline and Additionality Assessment Report; stakeholder consultation; E&S safeguards; SDG impacts. Detail is covered by the other researcher. — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **Scheduling.** The Audit Booking Calendar in MyPuro (announced 28 May 2026) lets suppliers "submit, manage, and track audit bookings". "Bookings can be submitted as early forecasts and updated or amended as schedules evolve." No lead times are stated. — [Mondo Visione: Audit Booking Calendar](https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/puroearth-launches-audit-booking-calendar-supporting-faster-time-to-revenue-fo-2026528/)
- **Facility certification marketing:** the VVB facility audit "remains valid for up to five years". — [Puro: Carbon credit certification](https://puro.earth/cdr-infrastructure/carbon-credit-certification/)
- Facilities certified under GR v3.x must move to the latest General Rules (GR v4.2 or later) and the latest methodology at their next crediting-period renewal (Clarification #016, 7 Aug 2025). — [Clarifications 06 Nov 2026](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Clarifications%20Puro%20Standard%2006%20Nov%202026.pdf)

### Inferences
- For GSC, read the "up to five years" on the certification page as the generic default. The methodology-specific 15-year crediting period in GSC 2.2.2 applies, and both GR 2.4.1 and CP 3.2.1 defer to the methodology.
- The Commitment Date clock usually starts when capture or injection equipment contracts are signed. Under GR 4.4 a cement-capture plus mineralisation project must be validated within 3 years of that date. If CP 5.3.9 applies, it has 5 years. This is the most important deadline to confirm with Puro in writing before signing major equipment contracts.

### Gaps
- No primary or third-party source gives a typical duration for the Facility Audit or Puro's internal review in weeks. The Audit Booking Calendar article gives no lead times.

## 6. How do output audits and CORC issuance work: reporting periods, Issuance Plus and timing rules?

### Takeaway
After validation, the supplier submits an Output Report (Monitoring Report) for each contiguous MP:
- **at least every 12 months**
- at most 18 months per report under GR 4.4; CP allows the **first** verification to cover up to 24 months
- the number of reports per year is capped by volume (1 per year below 10,000 CORCs, up to 12 at 60,000+)
- late by more than 12 months: suspension; by more than 18 months: withdrawal or deregistration and a fresh validation

Puro reviews the VVB report and issues whole CORCs to the supplier's account. Issuance Plus allows quarterly, monthly or even weekly audits and issuance for suppliers at ≥1,000 CORCs per audit.

### Cited Findings
- "An Output Report shall be submitted to each Production Facility at minimum once every 12 months throughout the Crediting Period. An Output Report may consider Production Facility performance for up to 18 months." A report more than 12 months late means suspension. Beyond 18 months means deregistration, and re-registration needs a new Facility Audit with "reevaluation of baseline and assumptions". The suspension lifts once an Output Report and an explanation are received (GR 2.4.2-2.4.3). — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **CP v1.2 rules:**
  - "Monitoring periods shall be contiguous and all Project Activities, once operational, must undergo verification at least every twelve (12) months" (5.4.1).
  - MPs must not overlap (5.4.2).
  - "The first Monitoring Period shall begin on the Crediting Period start date" (5.4.3).
  - The first verification may run "in tandem with the Validation audit upon request by the Operator" (5.4.4) and "may cover a period of up to 24 (twenty-four) months" (5.4.5).
  - Reporting delays: more than 12 months means suspended; beyond 18 months means withdrawn; re-entry needs a new Validation Audit (5.5).
  - Source: [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Combined audit (GR 2.3.4):** "The Issuing Body may choose to combine design validation and performance verification". The supplier then submits all facility and output documents in its first submission, and Puro runs a combined review. — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- The **Output Audit** "includes a desk study and a site visit … The site visit may be conducted as a remote site visit" (GR 2.3.2.3). Clarification #028 (21 Jul 2026) adds that, unless Puro requires one, the auditor decides whether a site visit is needed and "can deem that a site visit is not required for an Output Audit", given that "With Puro Issuance Plus Facilities are undergoing Output Audits more frequently: on quarterly, monthly or even weekly basis". — [Clarifications 06 Nov 2026](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Clarifications%20Puro%20Standard%2006%20Nov%202026.pdf)
- **Zero-output periods** (Clarification #025, 7 May 2026): an Output Report showing zero output must still be filed within 12 months. No audit is held until the next Output Audit. A zero report "cannot be amended later to indicate Output volume for that Monitoring Period". — same
- **Volume-based audit frequency** ("Increased Audit Frequency Under General Rules 4.4"), Output Reports per facility per rolling 365 days:
  - 60,000+ CORCs: 12
  - 30,000–59,999: 6
  - 20,000–29,000: 4
  - 10,000–19,000: 2
  - below 9,999: 1
  - Source: [Puro Fees](https://puro.earth/certified-carbon-credits/fees/)
- **Issuance mechanics:**
  - Puro issues one credit per verified tonne (CP 9.2.1), only after a successful Puro review of the Verification Audit Report (9.2.2), into the account of an approved Account Holder (9.2.3).
  - Credits are issued as whole integers. Any remainder under 1 t is carried into the next issuance, and the status stays "Verified" (9.2.9).
  - CORC attributes include: serial number, issuance date, program, methodology and version, durability label (CORC100+/200+/1000+), facility ID, name and location, host country and sector, and MP dates (9.2.6).
  - Source: [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **GSC point of creation:** "the moment when the CO2 Stream has been injected into the geological storage reservoir … and the data records thereof can be verified" (GSC 2.3.1). GSC storage must ensure no re-emission "for at least 1000 years" (3.2.1). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- **Vintage:** "The calendar year in which the Carbon Removal occurred". Where the MP spans years, the latest year is the vintage (GR Definitions). — [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **Issuance Plus:** launched 27 Jan 2026. "Issuance happens in batches, and suppliers can request a cycle when needed". Issuance still rests on completed third-party audits. It runs on MyPuro 2.0 and dMRV Connect, with a minimum of 1,000 CORCs per audit. — [Puro blog: Issuance Plus launch](https://puro.earth/our-blog/361-puro-earth-launches-puro-issuance-plus-to-enable-higher-frequency-of-carbon-credit-issuance-for-suppliers). On the certification page, "For eligible Issuance Plus suppliers, audits and issuance happen on-demand". — [Puro: Carbon credit certification](https://puro.earth/cdr-infrastructure/carbon-credit-certification/)
- **Beneficiary disclosure:** at the Operator's request, Puro can embargo disclosure of a beneficiary's identity for up to 12 months from issuance (CP 9.9.4). — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Publication and redaction:** the Verification Audit Report and Monitoring Report are published. Operators may redact personal data, commercially sensitive pricing and contract terms, and security-sensitive details. They may **not** redact equations, parameter values, calculation spreadsheets, baseline, uncertainty, leakage or methodology versions. Precise coordinates and raw datasets may be redacted conditionally, with approval. The public versions must be supplied at submission (CP 5.6.5-5.6.10). — same
- **Reversals:** notify Puro within 5 days of detection (CP 9.10.3; GR 6.7.4.1). For geological storage in the EEA or UK, where statutory allowance surrender applies, Puro takes no other remedial action (CP 9.10.7; GR 6.7.5.4). — same
- **Timing example (GSC, third-party):** a Norwegian biogas BECCS project certified under GSC Edition 2024 received "more than 700" CORCs, announced 21 Jul 2026, covering "its first four months of operation, from February to May 2026" (CO2 trucked to an offshore storage terminal). — [Mondo Visione, 21 Jul 2026](https://mondovisione.com/news/puroearth-certifies-worlds-first-permanent-carbon-removal-from-biogas-producti-2026721/)

### Inferences
- Under the GR 4.4 crediting-period definition, the **earliest creditable removal is the first day of the first MP, which also starts the crediting period**. There is no separate "pre-registration" crediting. Removals are claimable back to the MP start, as long as that MP sits inside the validated crediting period and the first report covers at most 18 months (GR) or 24 months (CP). Under CP 3.2.2, credits for any period before validation "shall be subject to a positive Validation outcome".
- Because the first verification can run with validation, a sensible plan for a GSC facility is: start injection, run a first MP of a few months, then submit a combined Facility + Output package. The BECCS example suggests roughly 2 months from MP end to issuance is achievable, though the project's pre-operation certification work is not disclosed.
- Below about 10,000 CORCs a year a facility gets one Output Report per year, unless it qualifies for Issuance Plus (≥1,000 CORCs per audit). Issuance Plus is the route to quarterly or monthly cash flow.
- The local spec's frequency tiers and integer-issuance rule are confirmed.

### Gaps
- It is not documented how Issuance Plus frequency relates to the volume-tier caps on the fees page. A search snippet (not verified by direct fetch) said "up to 12 issuance batches per year".
- No public statistic on time from Output Report submission to issuance.

## 7. What eligibility rules apply to a new facility?

### Takeaway
The main gates for a new GSC mineralisation facility:
- an approved methodology: GSC 2024 v5 explicitly covers dissolved-CO2 injection into ultramafic or mafic rock
- an **eligible CO2 source**: biogenic only. Clarification #022 confirms that biogenic CO2 from cement kilns co-firing coal and biomass is eligible.
- a **storage jurisdiction with a robust CCS legal framework**: US, EEA, UK and three Canadian provinces are accepted a priori. Anywhere else must prove it meets Table 1, and Puro decides.
- regulatory and financial additionality, with the 3- or 5-year validation window from the Commitment Date
- disclosure of other registrations, with no simultaneous registration and a 3-year bar after failing elsewhere
- biomass sourcing criteria
- Article 6: an NDC assessment, and a host-country Letter of Authorization if corresponding-adjusted use is sought

CRCF certification through Puro is not yet possible (scheme not recognised). CCS+ storage modules do not cover mineralisation.

### Cited Findings
- **CO2 source.**
  - Eligible: DAC, and biogenic CO2 from thermochemical treatment of biomass, waste incineration, biological treatment, and "other industrial processes" (GSC 3.2.3a).
  - Ineligible: "Any CO2 from fossil sources" and "Any CO2 (even biogenic) captured from activities relating to coal-fired electricity generation" (3.2.3b).
  - Mixed streams are allowed, but "only the biogenic fraction" is credited (3.2.5).
  - The CO2 stream must be ≥95% CO2 by volume (3.2.2).
  - Source: [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- **Cement-specific clarification #022 (30 Oct 2025):** "Rule 3.2.3.b, shall not be interpreted as making non-eligible for CORC issuance the use of biogenic carbon resulting from the co-fire of coal and eligible biomass in cement clinker production." — [Clarifications 06 Nov 2026](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Clarifications%20Puro%20Standard%2006%20Nov%202026.pdf)
- **Storage type:** "Injection of dissolved CO2: Injection of a CO2 Stream … dissolved in water or seawater (i.e CO2 charged water) into subsurface igneous (ultramafic, mafic, intermediate, or silicic) rock formations suitable for rapid mineralization". This requires immediate solubility trapping, with pressure at the point of entry above the bubble point. No caprock is needed. Storage in the water column or in man-made containers is not allowed (GSC 3.2.6). Sites need regulatory approval and permits (3.2.8-3.2.10). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- **Jurisdiction (GSC 3.2.11):**
  - Injection may take place "only … in jurisdictions with a robust legal framework".
  - (a) The law must not prohibit storage.
  - (b) Accepted a priori: USA, EEA states, UK, and Canada (Alberta, Saskatchewan, British Columbia).
  - (c) Any other jurisdiction must show it meets **Table 1**: a legal framework designed for permanent storage; a storage permit; site characterisation; a definition of an eligible CO2 stream; monitoring; periodic reporting; emergency and remedial response; closure and post-closure; and financial responsibility. The table cites EU CCS Directive and US 40 CFR 144/146 examples. "Puro.earth reserves the right to determine the eligibility of a legal framework", and the evidence is verified by the Facility Auditor.
  - Source: same
- **Additionality.** The activity must be "not required by existing laws, regulations, or other binding obligations" and must "convincingly demonstrate that the CO2 removals are a result of carbon finance" per the AAR (GSC 3.5.1; GR 6.5.1-6.5.4). The baseline must be project-specific and conservative, and is updated at every renewal (GR 6.5.2). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf); [GR v4.4](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/General%20Rules%204_4.pdf)
- **GSC baselines:** "bio-CCS Retrofit" or "bio-CCS New built". New-built requires the capture unit to be installed "within 48 months of the operational start of the biomass conversion facility" (GSC 3.4.3-3.4.4). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- **Prior consideration (Common Criteria CC 3.1.1, v1.0 28 May 2026):** carbon finance must be considered before commencement, and "The Operator shall submit the project for validation no later than two years after the activity start date". Later submissions need a justification validated by the VVB. Exceeding the window without justification is a Major NC. — [Common Criteria v1.0](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Common%20Criteria%20v1.0.pdf)
- **Design-validation window:** 3 years from the Commitment Date (GR 2.2.3; AAR 2.1.2), or 5 years for geological-storage projects (CP 5.3.9). See Q5.
- **Double registration and scheme hopping:**
  - "The Registry does not allow simultaneous registration of the same CO2 Removal activity with another carbon removal crediting program" (Clarification #019; GR 3.5.3.1).
  - Transfers in require non-overlapping MPs and a deregistration document (GR 3.5.3.3; CP 9.8.6-9.8.7).
  - The Operator must disclose certifications or attempts in the preceding 5 years (CP 9.8.20) and rejections in the previous 3 years (9.8.5).
  - It cannot be certified if it "has failed a certification audit under another certification scheme within the previous three (3) years" while that scheme still operates (9.8.21).
  - "Once Validated, project activities and carbon credits must remain exclusive to the Puro Certification Framework" unless deregistered (9.8.24).
  - Undisclosed dual certification means suspension, possible withdrawal and compensation (9.8.25).
  - Simultaneous registration for non-carbon credits (biodiversity, renewable energy) is allowed if disclosed (9.8.10).
  - Sources: [Clarifications 06 Nov 2026](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Clarifications%20Puro%20Standard%2006%20Nov%202026.pdf); [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Supply-chain double claims:** no other party in the chain may make removal claims (marketing, branding or footprint) unless CORCs are retired for that purpose. Parties may still report in inventories with disclosure (GSC 3.6.1). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- **Biomass:** origin, type and sustainability must be traceable per the latest Biomass Sourcing Criteria. Any share not demonstrated is excluded (GSC 3.7.1-3.7.2). The BSC 3.2 counterfactual rules become mandatory for crediting periods starting on or after 1 Jan 2029. Earlier crediting periods must comply from their first renewal on or after that date (Clarification #033, 6 Nov 2026). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf); [Clarifications](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Clarifications%20Puro%20Standard%2006%20Nov%202026.pdf)
- **Host country / Article 6 (CORSIA-relevant).** The supplier "shall evaluate whether the geological storage activity falls within the Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) … of the host country". If it does, it "shall request authorization of use for trading CORCs within the Article 6" from the designated authority, following Puro's Article 6 Procedures, to avoid double counting, including with CORSIA (GSC 3.6.2). — [GSC 2024 v5](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Geologically_Stored_Carbon_2024_5.pdf)
- **Letter of Authorization (CC 2.4.1-2.4.2).** Where Article 6 use is sought, the Operator must show at validation that the authorisation process with the host-country NDA is underway (or document engagement where no process exists). A valid Letter of Authorization is needed before "Article 6 Authorized Use" status is assigned at verification. Using voluntary-only credits under Article 6 without notifying Puro is a Critical NC. — [Common Criteria v1.0](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Common%20Criteria%20v1.0.pdf)
- **CRCF:** "Puro.earth has not been recognised as a Certification Scheme under the EU CRCF Regulation and will therefore only be able to certify Project Activities under that framework once approved" (CP 3.1). The Puro CRCF BioCCS methodology v1.1 (Nov 2026) is published. — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf); [Document Library](https://puro.earth/cdr-infrastructure/methodologies/document-library/)
- **CCS+:** CCS+ Program Annex v1.0 (approved 30 Sep 2026). Crediting period "5 years, renewable 4 times". It issues CCS+ Carbon Removal Certificates and Emission Reduction Certificates, and mixed streams need a Tool E split. Its storage module is "Module D – CO2 Storage in Saline Aquifers and Depleted Hydrocarbon Reservoirs". — [CCS+ Program Annex v1.0](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro_CP_CCS_Plus_Program_Annex_v1.0.pdf); [Document Library](https://puro.earth/cdr-infrastructure/methodologies/document-library/)
- **Transition between Puro programs:** issued but unretired credits can be cancelled and reissued under another methodology for which the activity holds a valid Validation Audit Report (CP 9.6). — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Mineralisation route under GSC:** the CO2 is liquefied for transport but must be **dissolved in water at or before injection** to meet GSC 3.2.6. This, together with the bubble-point pressure monitoring, is a design point to confirm early.
- **Jurisdiction is the likeliest Initial Qualification blocker.** If the storage site is outside the a-priori list, the supplier needs a legal memo mapping local law to every Table 1 row (permit, site characterisation, monitoring, reporting, remediation, closure, financial responsibility), with the auditor verifying it. Puro has discretion.
- **CCS+ is probably not a route for in-situ mineralisation** today: its storage module covers saline aquifers and depleted reservoirs only. This is inferred from the module title, not from a statement by Puro.
- **CRCF via Puro is not available yet.** A dual Puro + CRCF strategy would rely on CP 9.6 transitions once Puro is recognised.
- **It is unclear whether Common Criteria CC 3.1.1 (2-year submission window) binds a GSC facility** under the Puro Program. CP Table 1 says CC obligations arise only through methodology incorporation, and GSC 2024 v5 predates the CC. The conservative assumption is to submit for validation within 2 years of activity start, and within 3 years of the Commitment Date in any case.
- A host-country letter is not a general Puro requirement. It becomes mandatory only if the activity falls within the host's NDC and Article 6 or CORSIA use is sought, or (per GSC 3.6.2) where the NDC covers the activity. The supplier must document the NDC assessment either way.

- No public list of jurisdictions Puro has accepted under GSC 3.2.11(c).
- No explicit Puro statement on CORSIA eligibility of GSC CORCs was found.

## 8. What can a dMRV partner or consultant do (delegated access), and what must the supplier do itself?

### Takeaway
Consultants and project developers can prepare the PCN, PAD, LCA and audit package, and a dMRV partner can build and submit audit packages through dMRV Connect under access the supplier grants in MyPuro. The supplier must do the following itself:
- pass KYB/KYC through its authorised representative
- sign the Platform Agreement
- hold the registry account and the exclusive rights to the credits
- sign the legal attestation
- accept the allocated VVB
- make the reversal and material-change notifications

The VVB cannot have advised the project.

### Cited Findings
- "The Project Operator may designate another entity to represent the Operator and act on their behalf for the purposes of the Puro.earth framework" (CP 4.4.2). Transfers and retirements may be initiated by "the Account Holder, or a party authorized by the Account Holder" (CP 9.3.2, 9.4.3). — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- In Audit Preparation, "Project Developers and LCA consultancies listed on Puro's website can help". — [Puro CDR supplier FAQs](https://puro.earth/insights/puro-earth-faqs/cdr-supplier-faqs/)
- **dMRV Connect (Beta)** is "a machine-to-machine interface for automating audit package submission and other integrations". All endpoints require authentication. Users "reach out to Puro.earth to get onboarded, or share access to a dMRV Partner in the MyPuro Portal". The only environment listed is Production (Beta) at `https://api.portal.puro.earth/`. Support: tech-support@puro.earth. — [dMRV Connect overview](https://docs.api.puro.earth/dmrv/overview)
- Frameworks available through the API: Preliminary Assessment, Facility Audit and Output Audit. "Always use the latest version of each framework unless told otherwise by your account manager". — [dMRV Connect: Applying frameworks](https://docs.api.puro.earth/dmrv/guides/setting-up-monitoring-periods/applying-frameworks)
- MyPuro is a "supplier-first platform" with "digitized methodologies, and guided step-by-step certification" and an "Audit Booking Calendar". Login is at my.puro.earth. — [MyPuro](https://puro.earth/products/mypuro/)
- **VVB independence:** a VVB "shall not validate or verify a Project Activity for which it or an affiliated entity has provided or is providing advisory, technical, or design support" (CP 7.4.4). — [CP v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Supplier%20Documents/Puro%20Earth%20Certification%20Procedures%20v1.2%20FINAL.pdf)
- **Non-delegable supplier acts:**
  - KYC on the authorised representative with proof of signatory rights ([KYC v1.2](https://7518557.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/7518557/Puro%20Earth%20KYC-AML%20Policies%20V1.2.pdf))
  - signing the PA (CP 4.2.2)
  - exclusive legal right to the credits (CP 4.4.1)
  - the attestation "by an individual with legal signing authority within the CO2 Removal Supplier" (GR 2.2.4.3)
  - confirming acceptance of the allocated VVB (CP 5.2.2)
  - notifying reversals within 5 days (CP 9.10.3) and material changes "immediately" (CP 5.8.2)
  - Sources: as cited

### Inferences
- A dMRV partner's realistic scope:
  - assemble the Preliminary Assessment, Facility and Output frameworks and the LCA model in MyPuro or dMRV Connect
  - run the pre-submission consistency checks
  - manage the monitoring-period calendar against the 12/18-month and volume-tier rules
  - prepare public (redacted) document versions
- Because submitting an audit package through the API is itself the supplier's attestation, the partner should obtain a recorded sign-off from the supplier's legal signatory before each submission (this matches the local spec's recommendation).
- **The dMRV Connect methodology list could not be verified.** The local spec states that GSC is not among the methodologies exposed through dMRV Connect (its table lists Biochar 2022/2025, Terrestrial Storage of Biomass, Carbonated Materials and Microalgae). The public guide pages fetched on 14 Nov 2026 list no methodologies. Until Puro confirms GSC framework availability, plan for a MyPuro-UI-based (manual) GSC submission.
