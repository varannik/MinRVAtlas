## KEY INSIGHT: This is NOT Biochar — It's Geological CCS

The 44.01 methodology involves:
- Direct air capture OR industrial CO2 capture
- Transportation and injection into geological formations
- Permanent mineralisation (peridotite/basalt)
- Monitoring for leakage over time

This requires a completely different ruleset than biochar.

## PURO.EARTH CCS CHECKPOINTS (based on real submission)

### Category 1: Administrative & Legal (3 checkpoints)
  PURO-CCS-A-01: Operator agreements and contractual terms
  PURO-CCS-A-02: Company registration and legal standing
  PURO-CCS-A-03: Regulatory approvals (NOC from relevant authorities)

### Category 2: Additionality (2 checkpoints)
  PURO-CCS-B-01: Additionality assessment (financial/regulatory/barrier)
  PURO-CCS-B-02: Baseline scenario documentation

### Category 3: Environmental & Social Safeguards (5 checkpoints)
  PURO-CCS-C-01: Stakeholder engagement and FPIC process
  PURO-CCS-C-02: Environmental and social safeguards framework
  PURO-CCS-C-03: Risk assessment of CO2 removal activity
  PURO-CCS-C-04: Chemical management plan (MSDS, SOPs)
  PURO-CCS-C-05: Environmental and social impact assessments

### Category 4: Storage Site Qualification (4 checkpoints)
  PURO-CCS-D-01: Storage site characterisation and satellite evidence
  PURO-CCS-D-02: Regulatory No-Objection Certificates (FNRC/FEA)
  PURO-CCS-D-03: Reservoir modelling and injectivity report
  PURO-CCS-D-04: Legal framework for storage rights

### Category 5: Monitoring Plan (4 checkpoints)
  PURO-CCS-E-01: Capture and transportation monitoring plan
  PURO-CCS-E-02: ACE methodology monitoring plan (ACE2)
  PURO-CCS-E-03: Data systems and digitalization overview
  PURO-CCS-E-04: Uncertainty quantification methodology

### Category 6: Leakage Determination (2 checkpoints)
  PURO-CCS-F-01: GHG emission displacement calculation
  PURO-CCS-F-02: Energy procurement and Scope 2 emissions

### Category 7: Life Cycle Assessment (2 checkpoints)
  PURO-CCS-G-01: Life cycle assessment spreadsheet (XLSM/XLSX)
  PURO-CCS-G-02: LCA report (third-party verified)

### Category 8: Project Description (1 checkpoint)
  PURO-CCS-H-01: Complete project description
